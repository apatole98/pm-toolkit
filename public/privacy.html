Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 May 2026  ·  Effective immediately

PM Toolkit ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it.

1. Data We Collect

Data Why we collect it Stored where
Name & email address Account creation and login Supabase (encrypted)
Form inputs you type To generate your PM documents Supabase (your account only)
Generated document content To save and show your document history Supabase (your account only)
Usage count (docs/month) To enforce Free plan limits Supabase
Payment status (tier: free/pro) To determine your plan Supabase

We do not collect credit card or bank details — all payment processing is handled by Razorpay.

2. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to operate PM Toolkit:

  • Supabase — authentication and database. Your account data and documents are stored here. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Anthropic (Claude AI) — generates your documents. Your form inputs are sent to Anthropic's API to produce the document. Anthropic does not use API inputs to train their models by default. Anthropic Privacy Policy
  • Razorpay — processes payments for Pro plan. We receive only a confirmation of payment status, not your card details. Razorpay Privacy Policy
  • Netlify — hosts the website and serverless functions. Standard server access logs may be retained. Netlify Privacy Policy

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and maintain your account
  • To generate documents based on the inputs you provide
  • To track your monthly usage and enforce plan limits
  • To upgrade your account to Pro after a successful payment
  • To respond to support requests

We do not sell your data to any third party. We do not use your documents to train any AI model.

4. Data Retention

Your account data and documents are retained as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your data from our systems within 30 days.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS encryption)
  • Row-Level Security (RLS) in our database — you can only access your own data
  • API keys and secrets stored as server environment variables, never in frontend code

No system is 100% secure. If you believe there is a security issue, please contact us immediately.

6. Cookies

PM Toolkit uses minimal cookies — only those set by Supabase to maintain your login session (a JWT token stored in browser local storage). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the data we hold about you
  • Correction — update your name via the Account Settings page
  • Deletion — request that we delete your account and all associated data

To exercise any of these rights, email us at support@pmtoolkit.in.

8. Children's Privacy

PM Toolkit is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of PM Toolkit after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@pmtoolkit.in.
